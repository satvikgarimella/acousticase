# Constructed sound scene attribution

Constructed collage of separate field recordings. Not a recording of a real incident.

Highest mean target-group score in training folds per class; curated for demonstration, not evaluation.

Resampled to mono 16 kHz; 10 ms edge fades; one-second silent gaps; PCM16 export.

Source: [ESC-50 by Karol J. Piczak](https://github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50). Dataset license: [CC BY-NC 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/).

The complete upstream license and source credits are retained in `../evaluation/ESC50-LICENSE`.
